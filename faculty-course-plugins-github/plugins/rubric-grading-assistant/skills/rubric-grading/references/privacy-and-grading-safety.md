# Privacy and Grading Safety

Use this reference whenever a real student submission is involved.

## Before analysis

- Confirm that the instructor is using an institution-approved tool and account for the data involved.
- Prefer de-identified copies labeled with neutral aliases.
- Treat names, student numbers, email addresses, indirect identifiers, accommodation details, conduct records, and grades as potentially sensitive.
- Do not copy student information into filenames or reports.
- Do not open unrelated roster, gradebook, accommodation, or advising files.

If approval is not confirmed and identifiable information is present, stop before grading and ask for an approved or de-identified version.

## During analysis

- Base judgments only on the submitted work and supplied criteria.
- Avoid demographic, medical, disability, nationality, language-background, or intent inferences.
- Do not present an AI-writing or plagiarism conclusion. Point out only observable citation or attribution issues that the rubric explicitly requires, and leave misconduct decisions to the institution's established process.

## Output

- Use a neutral submission alias.
- Separate student-facing feedback from instructor-only review notes.
- Do not enter, transmit, or publish a grade.
- Remind the instructor that the proposed score requires human review.
