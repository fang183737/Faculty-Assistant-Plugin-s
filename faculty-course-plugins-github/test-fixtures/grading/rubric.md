# Fictional Rubric

| Criterion | Excellent | Developing | Incomplete | Points |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Problem definition | Specific, supported explanation | General or partly supported explanation | Problem is absent or unclear | 30 |
| Proposed change | Actionable and clearly connected to the problem | Plausible but connection or detail is limited | Change is absent or unrelated | 30 |
| Evaluation plan | Measurable evidence and a feasible comparison | Evidence is named but measurement is vague | No meaningful evaluation plan | 30 |
| Clarity | Organized and understandable | Some organization or clarity issues | Difficult to follow | 10 |

Use whole-number scores. Do not apply penalties outside these criteria.
